Bio

Tanner Price is a Visual Communication Design student at San Francisco State University. Originally from San Luis Obispo, California, he recently moved to the Bay Area to continue his education and focus more seriously on design. Being in a new environment has given him the opportunity to meet new people, experience a different creative community, and spend more time developing his design work. At San Francisco State, he is building his skills in visual communication while learning more about how design operates within larger cultural and historical contexts. He is especially interested in how typography, composition, and imagery can work together to communicate ideas clearly. Many of his projects focus on experimenting with layout and type to find simple but effective ways to present information. He enjoys paying attention to small details and figuring out how those details can strengthen the overall design. Outside of design, he spends a lot of his time surfing, playing guitar, and drawing. Growing up on the Central Coast made surfing a regular part of his life, and it remains one of his favorite ways to spend time away from the computer and out in nature. Being in the ocean is something he has always enjoyed, and it provides a balance to the more structured side of design work. Playing guitar and drawing are also creative outlets he returns to regularly. Both offer a more relaxed way to explore ideas and creativity without overthinking things too much. These interests often influence how he approaches his visual work. A few fun facts about him: his favorite food is a breakfast burrito and his favorite color is forest green.
