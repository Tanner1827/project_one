A Few People Who Inspire Me

Trey Flanigan

What I like about the work of Trey Flanigan is his stylistic use of thick, dark line work and minimal color palettes, which gives his illustrations a strong and recognizable look.

Robert Beatty

Robert Beatty creates a strong sense of depth and movement through shadowing, color choices, and detailed imagery.

Pavement

Pavement is an indie slacker-rock band that has changed how I listen to music with their chord progressions, lyrics, and album art.
